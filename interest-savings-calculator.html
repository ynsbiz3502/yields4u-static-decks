Disclosures

Illustration only. This calculator is for educational and illustrative purposes only. It is not tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice, and is not a recommendation of any lender, loan, product, or strategy. Consult a qualified tax, legal, and financial professional before acting.

Modeling assumptions. Results assume both balances are fully amortized in equal monthly payments over the same term at a constant annual rate. Actual interest will differ if the payment schedule, term, draw timing, or rate behavior differs from these assumptions.

Rates are not fixed or guaranteed. The rates shown are advisor-entered inputs intended for comparison only. They are illustrative, may exclude lender or service fees, and are not a guarantee of availability or execution. Floating rates reset with their underlying index and can rise. Confirm current terms directly with the provider.

Securities-backed lending — if applicable. If this calculator is used to compare a securities-backed loan or line of credit, borrowing against a portfolio pledges those assets as collateral. A decline in collateral value can trigger a maintenance or margin call requiring additional collateral or repayment on short notice, and the lender may sell pledged securities — possibly at a loss, at an inopportune time, and with tax consequences. Many such lines are demand facilities callable at any time. These risks are not reflected in the chart above.

Taxes. Whether interest is deductible depends on how the loan proceeds are used and on individual circumstances; it is not automatic. This tool does not calculate tax effects. Consult a tax advisor.

Comparison scope. This tool compares only two user-entered rates on a single balance and term. It does not evaluate all available financing options, the capital-gains or opportunity cost of selling versus borrowing, fees, or suitability. Other solutions may offer similar or greater benefit.

Advisor use. If presented to clients, the presenting advisor is responsible for ensuring the material complies with applicable advertising and communication rules — including SEC Marketing Rule 206(4)-1 and FINRA Rule 2210 — and for retaining required records.

Figures are estimates; rounding may cause minor differences.
