Before we run the numbers

Five questions — for us, together.

Take these in order before the data goes in. The real conversation starts here. There are no wrong answers.

  1. Before we talk about anything financial — what brought you in today? What were you hoping would come out of this conversation?

  2. If we could wave a wand and the tax piece were completely handled — what’s still worrying you?

  3. If this relationship went well — five years from now, we’re still working together — what would we have done for you that would make it worth it?

  4. Is there anything I haven’t asked you about that you were hoping to talk about?

  5. What would have to be true for you to look back and say “that was the best decision we made in retirement”?

Once you’ve worked through these together, continue to enter the numbers and generate the analysis.
